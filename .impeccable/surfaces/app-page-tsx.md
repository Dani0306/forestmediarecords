## Direction contract

THESIS: Forest forges emerging artists. The page is a forge floor: every artist is a workpiece under heat, every event is a heat on the schedule. It refuses the label template of a full-bleed artist hero over a row of event cards.

OWN-WORLD: forge black ground, anvil-gray steel panels, one heat ramp (black → cherry → orange → white heat) used as state, scale-black grain. Heavy stencil caps (Saira Stencil, width axis), tabular mono measurements (Martian Mono), chalk-ruled scales, hot-plate primary buttons with grain, cold outlined steel secondary buttons.

STORY: The visitor learns that Forest develops emerging artists in Medellín with Kick. They see the next event glowing, scan the agenda by temperature, meet the artists as workpieces, then watch on Kick or bring their own metal (a demo).
