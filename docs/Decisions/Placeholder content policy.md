# Placeholder content policy

## Context
No real events, Kick URLs, email or bios were available.

## Decision
Never invent facts. Sample data carries `sample: true` (visible "Ejemplo" / "Biografía de ejemplo" tags); missing links show "por conectar"; the demo form stays closed without an email.

## Consequences
Honest site from day one; swapping in real data needs only data-file edits ([[Content guide]]).
