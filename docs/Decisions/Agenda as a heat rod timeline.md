# Agenda as a heat rod timeline

## Context
The agenda was a carousel of photo cards over black glass, the same recipe as Trending, the artist stage and the streamers. The team found it too similar to the rest of the page.

## Decision
Draw the agenda as one rod of metal laid onto real time (picked by the team from three directions: heat rod, departure board, wall calendar). Today is a needle; the rod is cold behind it and carries the heat ramp in front of it, cooling at the agenda's own thresholds. Events are notches at their true dates; the chosen one opens out under the rod. On phones the rod becomes a vertical spine with the details inline.

## Consequences
- The section is now typographic and temporal, not photographic; the photo appears once, for the chosen event.
- The heat legend and the "Mostrar finalizados" toggle are gone: the rod is the legend, and the last three finished events stay on it, cold.
- Filters only appear when there is more than one event type.
- Very long ranges (100+ days) drop the day ticks; events on the same day stack on two shelves.
