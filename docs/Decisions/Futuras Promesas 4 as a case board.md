# Futuras Promesas 4 as a case board

## Context
The team named Futuras Promesas 4 the most important upcoming event and sent three posters (two competitors and a guest judge) in a detective "evidence board" style: pinned photos, red string, fingerprints.

## Decision
- A dedicated section right after the hero, plus `main: true` so the hero takeover announces it.
- The section borrows the posters' own motif, rebuilt in the forge world: a steel case board, prints hung from cherry pins, red string from each competitor to the guest judge, drawn in on scroll, an "Invitado especial" rubber stamp. The guest is centre stage because the team singled the jury out as a special guest.
- The hero takeover gained `gallery` (extra posters fanned behind the lead, opening once on load) instead of a made-up composite poster.

## Consequences
- With the posters printing no end time, the event lasts the default 2 hours in the agenda.
- After 7 Nov the hero goes back to the video and the section shows "Finalizado" until it is removed or turned into results.
