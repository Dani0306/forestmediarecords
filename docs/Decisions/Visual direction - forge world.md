# Visual direction - forge world

## Context
Impeccable direction round (seed `d28d4ec1`). The roll assigned a "live broadcast" direction; the team **adopted the "Forge scale shower" world** from the declined alternatives.

## Decision

## Consequences
Distinctive, ties to the idiom "forjar artistas". Recorded in `DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/surfaces/app-page-tsx.md`.
