# Top songs as sleeve bands

## Context
The first "Lo más escuchado" (one record, side A / side B with a switch) was too plain for the team, and they wanted both artists visible without switching, even if it means scrolling. The songs and covers should be the stars.

## Decision
One full-width band per artist, stacked, mirrored. Number one is a big sleeve with its record peeking out and a giant outlined numeral behind; two and three are smaller sleeves thrown at an angle. Each band is lit by its number one's cover. A ticker of the artists' names in outlined stencil runs across under the heading.

## Consequences
- The section is tall (about two screens on desktop), but every song and cover is visible without interaction.
- It depends on good covers; without them the sleeves print the title on steel, which reads fine but less wild.
- More than two artists still works (bands keep alternating sides).
