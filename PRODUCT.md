# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A label hub that serves several audiences at once, routing each to the right place:

- **Fans and audiences** in Medellín and LATAM who follow the roster, watch Kick streams and attend concerts and events.
- **Emerging artists** deciding whether to develop their career with Forest Media Récords.
- **Partners**: Kick, brands, venues and promoters judging the label as a collaborator.

## Product Purpose

The official site of Forest Media Récords, a music production company based in Medellín, Colombia, focused on developing emerging artists. It introduces the company, presents the artist roster, and gives detailed, current information about upcoming events, concerts, streams and other activity. Success means a visitor quickly understands who Forest is, finds what is coming up next, and takes the action that fits them: watch, attend, follow, submit or contact.

## Positioning

A Medellín label whose purpose is developing emerging artists, with streaming and content creation built into the model through support from Kick. Streams and content are a core channel for these artists, not an afterthought to releases.

## Operating Context

- Streams happen on Kick (label and/or artist channels).
- Live concerts and events take place in Medellín (venues still to be provided).
- Visitors mostly arrive from social media on mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual: Spanish by default, with an English toggle.
- Event, concert and stream listings must be data-driven (one structured source) so they are easy to update.
- Stack: Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4.
- Undecided: demo submission mechanism, contact channels, booking flow.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Forest Media Récords** (keep the accent on "Récords").
- Logo: `public/logo.png`, a chrome "FR" monogram with a stag head and tree roots on a black vinyl record label. It is the primary mark and must stay intact.
- Kick is a confirmed supporter and should be visibly credited.
- The user asked for a modern, striking site with high-end design and impressive animations.

## Product Principles

1. **What's next comes first.** Upcoming events, concerts and streams are the reason to come back; they must be current and easy to scan.
2. **Artists are the face.** The roster carries the identity; the label frames them rather than competing with them.
3. **Rooted in Medellín.** Local identity is a strength, not a detail.
4. **Stream-native.** Live and on-demand content on Kick sits alongside live shows as an equal channel.
5. **Truth over hype.** Show real dates, real artists and real links; leave a placeholder rather than inventing a fact.
